# Brand kit: five channels

Every file here is generated by `node scripts/brand.js` (In This Town's art is rendered from the 3D engine).
- `avatar.png`: 1024×1024 profile picture (crops fine to a circle on every platform)
- `banner.png`: 2560×1440 YouTube banner, with all text and logos inside YouTube's always-visible center area

Handles below are suggestions; I can't check availability. Try them in order and use the **same handle on all three platforms**.
Bio limits: TikTok 80 characters, Instagram 150, YouTube description 1000.

---

## 1. Theo Has 100 Days · `theo/`
**Handles:** @theohas100days · @theo.100days · @100daysoftheo
**TikTok bio (80):** His online gf lands in 100 days. Every lie must come true. YOU decide 👇
**Instagram bio:** Theo told his online girlfriend he's a 6'2" firefighter with abs. She lands in 100 days. 🫠 Top comment decides what he fixes. Want a cameo? Comment 👇
**YouTube description:** Theo, 23, has been dating Pixie, a girl he met in an online game, for two years. They've never seen each other's faces. She just booked a flight. Everything he ever told her about himself is a lie, and he has 100 days to make it true. Every day the most-liked comment decides what Theo does next, and commenters can get cast in the episodes. New episode every day.
