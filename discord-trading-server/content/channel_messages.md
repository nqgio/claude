## #rules

> # 📜 Community Rules
> 1. **Be respectful.** No harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks.
> 2. **No financial advice.** Everything here is educational. You are
>    responsible for your own trades.
> 3. **No spam / self-promo / shilling** without staff approval.
> 4. **No pump-and-dump, insider info, or market manipulation.**
> 5. **No sharing premium content.** Leaking paid channels = instant ban.
> 6. **Keep it in the right channel.** Use the categories as intended.
> 7. **No scams, impersonation, or DM advertising.** Staff will never DM you
>    first asking for money.
> 8. **Follow Discord's [ToS](https://discord.com/terms) and Community Guidelines.**
>
> ⚠️ **Risk disclaimer:** Trading involves substantial risk of loss. Signals
> and analysis are for educational purposes only and are **not** financial
> advice. Never risk more than you can afford to lose.
>
> By verifying you agree to these rules.
